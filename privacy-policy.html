Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

North Velocity Group ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website northvelocitygroup.com (the "Site"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have regarding your information.

Information We Collect

Information you provide directly. When you submit our contact form, we collect the information you enter — typically your name, email address, company, phone number, and message. This information is sent to our team via a third-party form processing service in order to respond to your inquiry.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, we use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our Site. This may include your approximate location (derived from IP address), device and browser type, pages viewed, time spent on the Site, and how you arrived at the Site (e.g., search engine, direct link). This data is aggregated and does not directly identify you by name.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies to collect the usage information described above. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings, or opt out of Google Analytics tracking using Google's Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to: respond to inquiries submitted through our contact form; understand how visitors use our Site so we can improve it; and maintain the security and proper functioning of our Site. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate this Site:

— Google Analytics, to measure Site traffic and usage. Google's own privacy practices are described at policies.google.com/privacy.
— FormSubmit, to process and deliver contact form submissions to our team. FormSubmit's privacy practices are described at their website.
— GitHub Pages, which hosts this Site.

Each of these providers may process data according to their own privacy policies, independent of North Velocity Group.

Data Retention

We retain contact form submissions for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your inquiry and maintain business records, after which they may be deleted. Analytics data is retained according to Google Analytics' standard retention settings.

Your Rights and Choices

You may request that we delete personal information you've submitted to us, correct inaccurate information, or tell you what information we hold about you, by contacting us using the details below. Depending on your location, you may have additional rights under applicable law (such as the GDPR or CCPA).

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information submitted through our Site, including serving the Site over HTTPS. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

Our Site is intended for business audiences and is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise any of the rights described above, contact us at counsel@northvelocitygroup.com or through our contact page.
